Nada · ندى

A gentle companion for Sjögren's syndrome

Nada (ندى — Arabic for "dew") is a free, bilingual Arabic/English app that helps you track dryness, fatigue and joint-pain symptoms, log your medications, learn, and prepare a summary for your care-team visits — all stored privately on your own device.

  • Daily symptom tracker — dry eyes, dry mouth, joint pain, and fatigue on a simple 0–10 scale.
  • Weekly ESSPRI check-in — the validated 3-item patient-reported index used in Sjögren's care.
  • Medication log — record what you take and when, with no dosing suggestions.
  • Education library — bilingual, plain-language articles on dryness care and when to seek urgent care.
  • Clinician summary — a printable report you can bring to your appointment.
  • Private by design — your data stays on your device unless you choose to export or print it.

Nada is an educational companion. It does not diagnose, predict flares, or recommend treatment or medication doses. Always discuss symptoms, test results, and treatment decisions with your care team.
