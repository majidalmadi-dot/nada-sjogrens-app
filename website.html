About Nada

Your data never leaves your device.

Nada (ندى) is an independent, educational companion for people living with Sjögren's syndrome. Everything you log is stored on your device by default — no account required to start, and it works fully offline.

Nada is a non-medical, educational companion. It does not diagnose, predict flares, or recommend treatment or medication doses. Always discuss symptoms, test results, and treatment decisions with your care team.

Nada is an independent, non-medical companion for personal tracking and education. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment and is not a substitute for professional care.
